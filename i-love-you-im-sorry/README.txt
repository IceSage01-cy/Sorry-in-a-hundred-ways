I LOVE YOU, I'M SORRY - how to use

1. Put your song in the assets folder as:   music.mp3
2. Put your photo in the assets folder as:  photo.jpg
   (The photo appears in the middle of the heart when every star is lit.
    The music button appears once music.mp3 is there.)
3. Double-click index.html to preview it in your browser.

OPTIONAL: open index.html in a text editor and fill in the settings near the
top of the <script> section: his name, your name, a memory you treasure, and
a TALK_LINK (for example an sms: link) that opens when he taps "Yes, let's talk".

TO SHARE IT: upload this whole folder (index.html + assets) to a free host such
as Netlify Drop or GitHub Pages, then send him the link. Keep the folder
structure the same so the photo and music still load.
